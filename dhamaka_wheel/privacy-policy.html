Information We Collect

2.1 Local Data (Stored on Device Only)

  • Coins, scores, streaks
  • Game progress
  • Settings (sound, theme)

This data never leaves your device.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device info
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

Ads may be personalized. Where required (EEA/UK), we request user consent before showing personalized ads.

Learn more: policies.google.com/privacy · adssettings.google.com

3

Google Play Games (Optional)

The app supports Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. This feature is optional but requires signing in with your Google account. We do not access or store your Google account data.

4

In-App Purchases

Dhamaka Wheel offers optional in-app purchases. All payments are securely processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store any payment information.

5

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email, or phone number
  • No precise location
  • No contacts, camera, or microphone data
  • No payment details
6

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • Google Play Billing (payments)
  • Google Play Games Services (leaderboards & achievements)
7

Data Retention

All game data is stored locally. You can delete it anytime by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

8

Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9

Security

Your data is stored securely on your device. We do not store any data on external servers.

10

Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app means you accept updates.
