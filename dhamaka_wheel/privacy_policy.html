Privacy Policy — Dhamaka Wheel

Effective Date: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dhamaka Wheel ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you download and play the game. Dhamaka Wheel is built to work entirely on your device, without requiring an account — this policy explains exactly what that means in practice.

1. Who We Are

Dhamaka Wheel is developed by Agalecha.
Contact: agalecha.dev@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Dhamaka Wheel does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any contact information.

2.1 Information Stored Only On Your Device

The following is saved locally on your device using Android's standard local storage, and is never sent to us or any server we control:

  • Coin balance, streak count, and round/score history
  • Sound, music, and dark/light theme preferences
  • Any nickname you type in to label players on the wheel board
  • A locally cached copy of puzzle content, so the game works offline

Because this data never leaves your device, we have no way to access, view, or recover it — clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it permanently and immediately.

2.2 Information Collected by Advertising

Dhamaka Wheel shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and general network/carrier information
  • Device type, operating system version, and general usage/interaction data with ads (e.g., which ads were shown or tapped)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review and control it through:

  • Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google's Ads Settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads menu (to reset or limit your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads)

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your precise location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Payment information (Dhamaka Wheel does not currently offer in-app purchases)
  • Any data tied to a user account, because the app has no accounts or sign-in

4. How Information Is Used

  • Locally stored data is used only to run the game itself — remembering your progress, coins, and preferences between sessions.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is used to show and measure ads, and to help prevent invalid ad traffic, per Google's own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Dhamaka Wheel is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details in Section 1 and we will address it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all gameplay data lives only on your device:

  • It is retained for as long as the app remains installed with its data intact.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage/cache in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app — no request to us is required.

Advertising data collected by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's own policies, linked in Section 2.2.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., under GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we do not collect personally identifying information or operate a backend server, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device (Section 7) or through Google's own advertising controls (Section 2.2). For anything else, contact us using Section 1.

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Security

Your gameplay data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own operating system security. We do not transmit or store it on any servers we operate, so there is no server-side breach risk for that data on our end.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes — for example, if we add an account system, analytics, or in-app purchases in the future. We will update the "Last Updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
