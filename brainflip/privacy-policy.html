Privacy Policy for Brain Flip

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Brain Flip ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application Brain Flip.

Family Friendly & Privacy First: Brain Flip is designed for mixed audiences (children, teens, and adults). We use a neutral age selection screen on first launch to determine appropriate ad treatment. For child users and unknown-age users, all ad requests serve non-personalized advertisements with child-directed treatment flags enabled (`tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true`) and maximum ad content rating restricted to G.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Age Group Selection: We collect your age category (e.g., 9-12, 13-17, 18+) via a neutral age gate to configure appropriate ad targeting and privacy settings. We do not store exact dates of birth or personal identity details.
  • Game Progress: We store your game progress (completed levels, earned stars, brain points, and bulb hint counts) locally on your device using SharedPreferences.
  • In-App Purchases: When you purchase items (such as hints or ad removal), payment processing is handled securely by Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your credit card or financial details.

Automatically Processed Data

  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Third-party SDKs (such as Google Mobile Ads) may process technical info (device model, operating system version, coarse IP address for general region-level ad delivery, and diagnostic/crash logs) strictly for ad delivery, frequency capping, and preventing fraud.
  • Non-Personalized Advertising Data: Ad requests sent by Brain Flip specify non-personalized ad delivery and child-directed treatment for child users. Google AdMob processes contextual info (such as general app category and coarse location) rather than personal profiling or cross-app behavioral tracking.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain core game features and save progress
  • Configure privacy and ad treatment based on user age group
  • Process in-app purchases via Google Play Billing
  • Display non-personalized advertisements (rated G)
  • Sync leaderboard scores & achievements via Google Play Games Services when voluntarily initiated by the user
  • Improve app performance, stability, and fix bugs

Data Storage & Accounts

  • Local Progress: Game level progress is stored directly on your device.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to play Brain Flip.

Third-Party Services

We use the following privacy-compliant third-party SDKs:

1. Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • Purpose: Display non-personalized advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded ads).
  • Data Handling: Ad requests for children and under-age users are marked for child-directed treatment (`tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true`) and restricted to content rating G. Personalized ads and interest-based user profiling are disabled.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

2. Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards & Achievements)

  • Purpose: Sync leaderboard scores and display game achievements.
  • Data Handled: Pseudonymized Player ID and game score submissions managed via Google Play, active only when user explicitly connects their Play Games account.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Play Services Policy

3. Google Play In-App Billing

  • Purpose: Secure processing of digital purchases.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Play Terms

Advertising & Privacy Controls

Brain Flip serves non-personalized ads only. Advertisements are selected based on contextual information (such as the app topic or approximate general location) rather than individual user interest profiling or cross-app tracking.

Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & Play Families Policy)

Brain Flip is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We strictly comply with Google Play's Families Policy, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and GDPR-K:

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13.
  • We do not use behavioral remarketing or targeted advertising.
  • AdMob requests are restricted to maximum content rating G.

Data Security

All data transmitted between Brain Flip and third-party services (AdMob and Play Services) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

Your Rights & Data Management

You have full control over your data:

  • Clear Data / Delete Progress: Clear app storage via Android Settings > Apps > Brain Flip > Clear Data, or simply uninstall the application.
  • In-App Purchases: Manage your purchases anytime directly through your Google Account in the Play Store.
