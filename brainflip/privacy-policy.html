🧠 Privacy Policy for Brain Flip

Last updated: Jun 9, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Brain Flip ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Game Progress: We store your game progress, including completed levels, earned stars, brain points, and bulb counts locally on your device using SharedPreferences.
  • In-App Purchases: When you make purchases, payment information is processed by Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment details.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Game statistics, session duration, and feature usage.
  • Advertising Data: Our advertising partners may collect data for personalized ads (see Advertising section).

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the game functionality
  • Save your game progress
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Improve user experience and game features
  • Analyze app performance and fix bugs

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Game progress is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • No Account Required: Brain Flip does not require account creation or login.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Google AdMob

  • Purpose: Display advertisements
  • Data Collected: Advertising ID, device information, IP address, app usage data
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

Google Play In-App Billing

  • Purpose: Process in-app purchases
  • Data Collected: Purchase history, payment information (processed by Google)
  • Privacy Policy: Google Play Terms

Advertising

We display ads through Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising by:

  • Visiting Google Ad Settings
  • Adjusting your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization)

Children's Privacy

Brain Flip is a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (uninstall the app or clear app data)
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Request information about data collection practices

Data Retention

  • Game Progress: Stored locally until you uninstall the app or clear data
  • Purchase History: Maintained by Google Play Store according to their policies

International Data Transfers

Information collected by third-party services may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
